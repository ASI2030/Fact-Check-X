# Fact-Check-X v1.1.10 acceptance record

Fact-Check-X v1.1.10 closes the remaining report-separation and staged-interaction issues in the frozen release scope. It adds no platform, scoring metric, or unrelated workflow.

## Accepted behavior

1. Stage 2 presents direct answers and supplemental references as separate, prominent regions in both the overview and the per-knowledge-point detail. A small badge inside a mixed table is not treated as separation.
2. Stage 3 contains only the readable authoritative answer, its evidence boundaries, and a one-column authoritative source index. Per-platform verdicts, A1/A2 scoring, and supplemental-reference evaluation remain outside this report.
3. Stage 4 consumes the locked Stage-3 result and presents one per-knowledge-point evaluation area with distinct direct-answer and supplemental-reference subsections. It does not recompute or rewrite the authoritative answer.
4. Each stage emits an independently openable report and requires the user's explicit acknowledgement before the next stage. An initial request to run the complete workflow, carrier behavior, or a full-auto option cannot acknowledge later stages.
5. DeepSeek and Tencent Yuanbao references are not treated as source evidence until their target pages are opened and the source body is captured. Answer-page context, blocked acquisition, and failed acquisition remain distinct states.

## Verification evidence

- The same real Shenzhen spouse-settlement policy case was evaluated with DeepSeek and Tencent Yuanbao across all four stages, producing 11 atomic knowledge points without changing the underlying answer or authority decisions during report regeneration.
- DeepSeek captured 7 of 7 referenced source bodies. Tencent Yuanbao captured 64 of 67; one blocked acquisition and two failed acquisitions remained explicit and were not presented as source text.
- Stage 2 contains four ordered role regions: direct-answer overview, supplemental-reference overview, direct-answer details, and supplemental-reference details.
- Stage 3 contains no per-platform verdict table, A1/A2 score, or supplemental-reference evaluation. Stage 4 contains one locked detail area with direct-answer and supplemental-reference subregions.
- Desktop and mobile renders of all four reports were inspected with no page-level horizontal overflow.
- Eighteen Python package/module checks and ten JavaScript collector checks passed. A clean package installation prepared its runtime from zero and passed the complete workflow, checkpoint, platform, Trusted Search, governance, and golden-gate tests.
- The frozen golden regression gate passed 18 of 18 cases and covered 36 of 36 feedback identifiers, with no open high-priority lifecycle item.
- Two independent public builds and two independent SkillHub builds were byte-for-byte reproducible. Archive path and sensitive-content scans found no forbidden runtime state, credentials, or user-specific paths.
