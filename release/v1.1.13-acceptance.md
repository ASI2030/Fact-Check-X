# Fact-Check-X v1.1.13 acceptance record

Fact-Check-X v1.1.13 aligns platform scoring and report presentation with the September 12 product decision. It does not add a platform, external model API or workflow stage.

## Accepted behavior

1. Coverage measures whether a platform answered a valid direct knowledge point. An answered claim counts as covered even when its evidence remains insufficient; evidence sufficiency is measured independently.
2. A direct knowledge point is removed from both coverage and accuracy denominators only when every selected platform either omitted it or has insufficient evidence for it.
3. An expected official-source exemption may conservatively downgrade into an actual Trusted Search request. The inverse shortcut, which would skip a required search, remains blocked.
4. Raw `fabricated` and `unverified` categories remain in structured data for audit. User-facing reports combine both as “疑似误导”, move those claims into the supplemental-reference risk area, and keep them out of deterministic answers.
5. Serious and suspected misleading supplemental claims are highlighted near the top of the stage-four report. Long per-platform evidence is folded by default and remains fully readable on demand.
6. If required source acquisition or retrieval fails technically yet the workflow continues, every generated stage report contains a prominent technical notice describing the incomplete action and its possible impact.
7. Qwen reference URLs are opened and their source bodies captured before they are treated as textual evidence, matching the existing DeepSeek and Tencent Yuanbao behavior.
