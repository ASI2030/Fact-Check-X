# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be safe, respectful and useful.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on work and evidence;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and publication of private information;
- respect security embargoes, licenses, trademarks and platform terms;
- use synthetic data in public reports and reproductions;
- accept maintainers' good-faith moderation decisions.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these rules and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns through a private repository contact channel rather than a public issue when disclosure could harm someone.
